About TGM

Built by people who have run the finance,
not just reviewed it.

TGM Accounting & Auditing LLC is a Dubai mainland firm founded in 2026 by a Chartered Accountant and a career banker. We bring two decades of finance leadership from inside operating businesses in the UAE, India, Tanzania, Zambia and Mozambique to owner-run companies that need numbers they can trust.

Our story

Two careers on opposite sides of the table.

“Your system says you made a profit. Your bank may disagree.”

As Group CFO and Finance Manager, Yogesh Trivedi watched owners make big decisions on reports nobody had tested. As a relationship and credit officer at UAE banks, Anil Chowhan watched good businesses turned down because their numbers could not survive a credit review.

Most owners do not need more reports. They need someone who checks whether the reports are true, fixes what is not, and stands beside them when the bank, a buyer or the FTA starts asking questions. That is the firm we built.

Because so many UAE businesses trade with and operate in Africa, TGM works on both sides of the Indian Ocean — led by people who have closed books in Dar es Salaam and Maputo, faced the local tax authorities, and worked with local teams in Swahili and Portuguese.

The founder's journey

From office boy to Group CFO.

Twenty-five years, every rung of the ladder — and the reason TGM respects the basics.

  1. 2001

    Office boy

    Joins a Chartered Accountant's practice in Ahmedabad. Studies B.Com in Gujarati medium by day, learns the books by night.

  2. 2005

    CA exams, self-study

    Clears CA PE-II at the first attempt without coaching, while still working in the practice.

  3. 2008

    Chartered Accountant

    Qualifies with ICAI and adds an M.Com.

  4. 2009

    Finance Manager

    Karnavati University, India — then Finance Controller at Acer Petroleum, Tanzania.

  5. 2012

    Finance Controller

    Export Trading Group, Tanzania — VAT, payroll taxes and social security with no adverse findings across Big 4 audits.

  6. 2016

    Finance Manager

    Sharaf Group, Tanzania and Mozambique — 80% input VAT recovered on a capital project; borrowing moved to USD.

  7. 2021

    CFO

    Eaglite General Trading, Dubai — three-country trading group. Fractional CFO for Lake Trans Logistics across five East African countries.

  8. 2025

    Group Finance Manager

    Joga Ram Group, Dubai — building materials, joinery and fit-out; group ERP rollout and receivables cut from 150+ to under 120 days.

  9. 2026

    Founder, TGM

    Opens TGM Accounting & Auditing LLC in Dubai with Anil Chowhan, formally launched on Deepawali.

That climb shapes how TGM works: respect for the basics, patience with the detail, and every number earned.

Our vision

Our values

Our name is our standard.

TGM stands for Transparency, Governance and Materiality — three words every owner, banker and auditor understands, and the three tests we apply to all our work.

T

Transparency

We tell you what your numbers say, including what you would rather not hear. Every finding is in writing and valued in dirhams.

G

Governance

Controls, approvals and monthly checks that protect the owner's money when nobody is watching.

M

Materiality

We focus on what moves your money. Small issues are noted; material issues are fixed first.
