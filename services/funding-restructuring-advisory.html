Scope. Funding decisions, rates and terms are always the lender's. We advise and prepare; the facility agreement is signed directly between you and the lender. Our fee is agreed with you in writing before work starts and includes a success fee, payable by you, when the facility is approved. We take no commission from lenders. In restructuring we support you, your lawyers and any appointed trustee or liquidator; we do not act as trustee or liquidator ourselves.

What UAE lenders usually look for

  • Usually two to three years of trading history
  • Audited financial statements (lenders prefer three years)
  • Bank statements that support declared turnover
  • A clear purpose and repayment source for the funding
  • Clean UBO, AML and KYC documentation

Every lender sets its own criteria. Not ready yet? A Finance Health Check shows what to fix first.

Who it's for

  • SMEs with 2–3 years of trading looking for a first or larger facility
  • Contractors and traders whose cash is tied up in receivables or stock
  • Profitable businesses with a cash crunch or pressure from banks and suppliers
